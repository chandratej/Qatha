Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026 · Applies to the Katha reader app, Creator Studio, gateway, and katha.in surfaces.

1. Who we are

Katha (“we”, “us”) is a Telugu-first digital storytelling platform. Contact for privacy and grievances: grievance@katha.in (we aim to acknowledge within 72 hours).

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email and/or Google account identifiers when you sign in; optional display name.
  • Reading: story/chapter progress, preferences (font, theme), device id for session security.
  • Payments: subscription status and Razorpay payment references (we do not store full card/UPI credentials).
  • Creators: story content, pen name, payout UPI details you submit, legal consents.
  • Technical: IP address, app version, crash/diagnostics logs necessary to run the service.

3. How we use data

  • Provide reading, publishing, moderation, and subscription services.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce content policy, and process legal claims.
  • Improve product quality (aggregated analytics; no sale of personal data).

4. Legal basis & DPDP

We process personal data to perform the contract for our services, for legitimate security interests, and where required with your consent (e.g. creator agreement, marketing opt-ins). You may withdraw optional consents in Settings or by emailing grievance@katha.in.

5. Sharing

We use processors for hosting (e.g. Supabase), payments (Razorpay), and optional email/auth providers. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users’ safety.

6. Retention & deletion

Account and reading data are retained while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, email grievance@katha.in from your registered address with subject “Data deletion request”. We will confirm and complete deletion within a reasonable period except where law requires retention (e.g. payment records).

7. Children

Katha is not directed at children under 13. Stories may include mature themes; age ratings are shown when set by creators.

8. Contact

Grievance officer / privacy: grievance@katha.in
