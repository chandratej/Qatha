# Katha — Creator content template

**Audience:** authors onboarding to Creator Studio  
**Last updated:** 26 July 2026  
**Source:** Content format specifications (platform guidance)

This is a **craft template**, not a rulebook. **Chapter length never blocks publish** — write as short or long as the story needs. Recommended bands below are guidance only.

---

## 1. Serialized Story (primary format)

**Confidence: High** — recommended chapter length for serials (not a publish gate).

| Guidance | Value |
|----------|--------|
| Recommended range | **1,000–1,500 words** |
| Publish floor / ceiling | **None** — any length is allowed |
| Full arc length | Often **50–200** chapters |
| Before first launch | **15–20** chapters written if you can (buffer for cadence) |
| Update cadence | **3–5** chapters/week while active |
| Character ceiling | **None** |

### Editorial tips

- End chapters on a cliffhanger or open question when it serves the story.
- Aim for **1,000–1,500 words** when it fits the scene — shorter or longer chapters still publish.

### Cadence

> Target **1,000–1,500 words** per chapter (recommended), updated about **3–5×/week** while the series is active. Publish any length.

## 2. Short Story

**Confidence: Low — early guidance, subject to change** after the first alpha writers submit natural lengths.

| Guidance | Soft target (placeholder) |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Length | **1,500–7,500** words |
| Structure | **One** piece (not multi-chapter serial) |
| Role | New-author testing, contests, acquisition — not the main revenue format |

---

## 3. Short Story Collection

**Confidence: Low — early guidance, subject to change**

| Guidance | Soft target (placeholder) |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Bundle size | About **5+** pieces under one title/cover |
| Per piece | Same band as Short Story above |
| Role | Discoverability for standalones |

---

## 4. Flash Fiction

**Confidence: Low — early guidance, subject to change**

| Guidance | Soft target (placeholder) |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Length | **300–1,000** words |
| Structure | Single piece |
| Role | Shareability and contests — not a revenue format |

---

## 5. What we do **not** treat as authoring formats

| Idea | Policy |
|------|--------|
| Full-length novel upload | **Not** a separate tool. Completed serials (50+ chapters, status Completed) can appear as **binge bundles** in discovery. |
| Interactive / branching length specs | **No** official branch-count or per-branch word specs yet. |
| Epistolary / chat formal length | **No** official episode word specs yet — write naturally. |

---

## 6. Discovery (invisible to you as a “requirement”)

Readers’ discovery layer may group stories by published chapter depth:

- **≥ 20 published chapters** → treated as **Serialized** on discovery shelves  
- **Fewer than 20** → eligible for **collection-style** grouping  

This does **not** rewrite your format choice at creation and is **not** a publish gate.

---

## 7. What never happens

- No “X words required” red gates for chapter publish  
- No streak/guilt penalties for missing soft targets  
- No coins or chapter-chopping pressure  

---

## 8. Open validation (founders / product)

Short Story, Collection, and Flash numbers will be revisited after ~5 alpha writers’ natural submission lengths. Completion rate and re-read behavior at 800–1,500 words/chapter will confirm or adjust the serial band for Katha’s audience.
